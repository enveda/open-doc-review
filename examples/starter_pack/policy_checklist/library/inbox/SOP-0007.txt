Title: Issuing controlled copies of procedures
Owner:
Effective date: 2026-03-01                       Next review date: 2025-03-01

1. Purpose
   To describe how controlled copies of approved procedures are issued and withdrawn.

2. Scope
   All approved procedures held by the document-control office of Example Buyer Ltd.

3. Definitions
   Controlled Copy: a numbered copy of an approved procedure, issued to a named holder and
   recorded in the issue log.
   Issue Log: the register of every Controlled Copy issued and withdrawn.

4. Procedure
   4.1 On approval of a new version, print one Controlled Copy for each holder on the
       distribution list and record each in the Issue Log.
   4.2 Withdraw every master copy of the previous version and record the withdrawal in the
       Issue Log.
   4.3 Destroy withdrawn copies, or mark them "SUPERSEDED" if they must be kept.

5. Records
   The Issue Log is kept for 5 years.
