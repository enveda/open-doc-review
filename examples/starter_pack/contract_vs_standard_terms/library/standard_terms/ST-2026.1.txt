STANDARD SUPPLIER TERMS                          Version: ST-2026.1
Example Buyer Ltd - approved contract positions for supplier agreements
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Limitation of liability. Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid or payable in the
   12 months before the claim.
2. Term. The initial term is at most 36 months. No automatic renewal: any renewal is agreed in writing.
3. Governing law. The agreement is governed by the laws of Exampleland, and the courts of Exampleland
   have exclusive jurisdiction.
4. Confidentiality. Confidentiality obligations continue for 5 years after the agreement ends.

Deviations from these positions need approval from the contracts team before signature.
